Psychometrics for JavaScript and TypeScript, validated against R: classical test theory, item response theory, cognitive
diagnosis (G-DINA, DINA, DINO, A-CDM, LLM and R-RUM), adaptive, diagnostic adaptive and multistage testing, item and
person fit, differential item functioning for dichotomous and polytomous items, linking and equating, including
equating through common items, IRT observed-score equating and standard errors by bootstrap and the delta method,
score scaling and prediction, simulation, standard setting, rater agreement and rater effects by the many-facet Rasch
model, and response times.
