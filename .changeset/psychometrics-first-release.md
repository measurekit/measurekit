Psychometrics for JavaScript and TypeScript, validated against R: classical test theory with the value of subscores,
item response theory, including multidimensional models with exploratory and confirmatory calibration, rotation and
scoring, cognitive diagnosis (G-DINA, DINA, DINO, A-CDM, LLM and R-RUM), adaptive, diagnostic adaptive, multidimensional
adaptive and multistage testing with testlets, item and person fit, differential item functioning for dichotomous and
polytomous items, linking and equating, including equating through common items, IRT observed-score equating and
standard errors by bootstrap and the delta method, score scaling and prediction, dimensionality by polychoric
correlations and parallel analysis, explanatory item response models, generalizability theory, Mokken scale analysis,
simulation, standard setting, rater agreement and rater effects, including the many-facet Rasch model, and response
times with the joint model of speed and accuracy.
