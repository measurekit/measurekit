The foundation of `@measurekit/psychometrics` and `@measurekit/knowledge-tracing`: `MeasurekitError` and
`DataError`, the checks of arguments, the seeded random number generator `createRandom` and the numerics that
both packages use: the tails of the chi-square and Student's t distributions, Cohen's kappa, BFGS minimization and
the Fisher-Yates shuffle, and the category probabilities of logistic, graded and partial credit items.
